# Dataset attribution

Chen, D. (2015). *Online Retail* [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5BW33

The UCI dataset is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0). It contains 541,909 transaction lines from a UK-based online retailer, dated 1 December 2010 to 9 December 2011. The raw workbook is downloaded by `src/analyze.py` and is intentionally excluded from Git.
