# Data quality and definitions- Published file contains 552,073 trip records; this analysis keeps 548,371 unique, timestamped trips from 2026-01-01 00:00:26 through 2026-03-31 23:59:47 (Q1 2026 only).- Duplicate Trip_Id rows, invalid/missing timestamps and trips under 1 minute or over 4 hours are removed. This is a transparent analysis filter to reduce likely dock/recording errors; it may also exclude some genuine extreme trips.- Time variables use the local timestamps supplied by the City. No member-to-casual causal inference is made.- Quarterly winter data are not representative of a full-year or peak-summer profile; compare same months across years before staffing or station decisions.- Top station pairs count observed origin-destination pairs only; they do not measure capacity, bike availability, route distance, or station service quality.
