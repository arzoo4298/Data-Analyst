## Featured project

### Retail Sales & Customer Performance Dashboard

An end-to-end analysis of 541,909 UCI Online Retail transactions, including data cleaning, sales trends, repeat purchase behavior, heuristic customer segments, country and merchandise performance, and dashboard deliverables.

**Snapshot:** £10.67M recorded line revenue · 19,960 invoices · 4,338 identified customers · 65.6% repeat-customer rate.

- [Project walkthrough and source code](retail-sales-customer-performance/)
- [Excel dashboard](retail-sales-customer-performance/Retail_Sales_Customer_Performance.xlsx)
- [Monthly sales chart](assets/monthly_sales.png)
- [Customer segments chart](assets/customer_segments.png)

Recorded sales are positive quantity × unit price on non-cancelled lines, including qualifying postage/service lines. The source ends December 9, 2011; that final month is partial. Customer groups are descriptive RFM heuristics, not causal claims.
