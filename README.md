## Featured project

### Retail Sales & Customer Performance Dashboard

An end-to-end analysis of 541,909 UCI Online Retail transactions. The project cleans the source data, quantifies sales and repeat purchase behavior, builds heuristic customer segments, analyzes country and merchandise performance, and provides SQL, Python, Power BI starter files, and an Excel dashboard.

**Snapshot:** £10.67M recorded line revenue · 19,960 invoices · 4,338 identified customers · 65.6% repeat-customer rate.

### Business questions addressed

- What happened to sales and average order value over the year?
- How much of sales is associated with identified repeat customers?
- How concentrated is sales by country, and which products lead merchandise revenue?
- Which customer groups could merit further retention analysis?

### Key result

Recorded line revenue reached about £1.51M in November 2011. The UK contributed about £9.03M (84.6%) of all recorded line sales. 2,845 of 4,338 identified customers purchased on multiple invoices. December 2011 is partial, and RFM labels are descriptive heuristics rather than causal or predictive claims.
